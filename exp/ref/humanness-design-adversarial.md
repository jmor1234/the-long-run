# Design: adversarial

## Diagnosis
The bots are not badly tuned; they are missing three whole dimensions that humans have and this code does not: a **sizing dimension** (six pure-`pot` formulas, two of which collapse — the `Math.max(6,…)` floor at line 737 makes every open literally 6, and `applyAction`'s legal-minimum clamp at line 1099 turns every 3-bet into 10 — so the observable vocabulary is one open size, one 3-bet size, two postflop sizes, identical across all five personas), an **error dimension** (the table's highest-frequency decision, postflop call/fold at lines 816-822, is a deterministic step function with a 1.3-point equity spread across all five styles, so the code is structurally incapable of the bad calls and bad folds the judges looked for), and a **time dimension** (`style` is a frozen module const, ctx carries no hand index, no result history, no self-state, and `endHand` persists only `stack` and `reads.hands`, so nothing can drift). On top of that, the reasons are 18 template literals welded to `return` statements with the RNG printed verbatim in 73% of them — phrasing count is pinned to branch count by construction, which is why one voice wears five hats. The fix is four additive layers inside the coded policy: per-seat sizing fingerprints plus jitter, a soft (frequency-based) call boundary plus per-persona misreads, two decaying public-outcome mood counters plus a single nemesis slot, and a composed phrase bank replacing inlined strings. All stay pure functions of ctx; the new state is public-outcome-derived, written in `endHand`, and read only through ctx.

## Mechanisms
### M1 — Sizing fingerprints + chip-granular jitter (kills the two collapse points) [small]
Add four sizing dials to each `BOT_STYLES` entry (poker-trainer.html:831-843): `sizeOpen` (BB multiple: nit 3.6, solid 2.6, maniac 3.3, selective 3.0, station 2.2), `sizeBet` (base pot fraction: nit 0.55, solid 0.62, maniac 0.80, selective 0.72, station 0.42), `sizeVar` (jitter half-width: 0.10/0.12/0.20/0.12/0.18), `roundBias` (0.15-0.55, probability of snapping to a 5/10/25 multiple). Add `bb`, `currentBet`, `myBet`, `aggName` to the ctx built at step() lines 1153-1162 (all public; `currentBet`/`myBet` are already injected by exp-harness for the experiment, so shipping them removes a harness rewrite). Add a dedicated `const sizeRoll = Math.random()` at the top of botDecide, independent of `roll`, so size never correlates with which branch fired. New pure helper in the BOT POLICY section: `chipRound(x, r, style)` — nearest 1 below 20 chips, nearest 2 below 60, nearest 5 above, and with probability `style.roundBias` snap to the nearest 10 (or 25 above 100); with probability 0.06 leave a deliberately odd value (x+1..+3). Rewrite the six sizing sites: line 737 open becomes `chipRound(bb*style.sizeOpen*jit + deadMoney, …)` where `jit = 1 + (sizeRoll-0.5)*2*style.sizeVar` and `deadMoney = Math.max(0, pot - 1.5*bb)` (so limpers add chips) — the `Math.max(6,…)` floor is deleted; lines 761/770 3-bets become `chipRound((currentBet*(inPosition?3.0:3.6)*styleFactor + deadMoney)*jit)`, which lands well above `currentBet+minRaise` so the line-1099 clamp stops being the de-facto sizer; lines 793/807/812 become `chipRound(pot * style.sizeBet * bucketFactor * streetFactor * jit)` with bucketFactor {strong 1.15, good 1.00, marginal 0.82, semi-bluff 0.90} and streetFactor {flop 1.0, turn 1.05, river 1.15}. Net effect: opens span roughly 5-9 with a per-seat lean, 3-bets 14-24, and postflop sizes from adjacent buckets overlap instead of naming the hand.

Closes: Tell 1, 'INHUMAN UNIFORMITY — every open is exactly 7 chips; no sizing variation ever; robotic min-raise ladders.' Both cited artifacts are removed at their actual causes rather than papered over: the constant open dies with the `Math.max(6,…)` floor, and the min-raise ladder dies because policy sizes now exceed the legal minimum so the clamp never binds. Fresh-judge check: the residual pattern is a *stable per-seat lean* (Dunn opens small, Vera opens big) with chip-round numbers — that reads as five people with habits, which is also the readable tell the trainer wants to teach, not as an RNG.

### M2 — Soft call boundary + per-persona misread (manufactures costly, characterful mistakes) [moderate]
Two changes. (a) Replace the deterministic step at lines 817-822 with a frequency draw: `const margin = effective - need - edgeNeed; const pCall = clampFreq(1/(1+Math.exp(-margin/style.temp)))` with `temp` per persona (nit 0.05, solid 0.07, maniac 0.10, selective 0.06, station 0.13), then `if (Math.random() < pCall) call else fold`. Expectation stays near the old boundary; what changes is that close spots genuinely mix and the tails produce real errors at persona-specific rates. This also *increases* §3.4 compliance — ARCHITECTURE.md already flags this branch as the file's honest exception. (b) Add `style.misread` (nit -0.05 undervalues, solid 0.00, maniac +0.06, selective +0.02, station +0.09 overvalues, all with a ±0.05 noise term from a dedicated draw) applied once to `s` at line 776 before bucketing: `const sHat = clamp01(s + style.misread + (misreadRoll-0.5)*0.10)`. Because `sHat` feeds bucket, bet frequency, sizing and the call boundary together, a bot that misjudges a hand plays it wrongly and *consistently* wrongly — which is what a human error looks like. Keep the true `s` for nothing else; the reason text quotes `sHat` so the stated reason remains truthful to the decision that was actually made. Additionally widen the recreational-entry paths the panel counted: raise `limp` to nit 0.02 / selective 0.06 (currently hard 0.00, which is why three of five bots cannot enter a pot without raising), widen the station's limp band from `thr*2.2` to `thr*3.0`, and replace the unconditional fold at line 772 with a `style.loose`-gated curiosity call (nit 0.02, solid 0.05, maniac 0.14, selective 0.03, station 0.22) guarded by `toCall <= 0.06*myStack`.

Closes: Tell 2, '27 hands of perfect raise-or-fold with zero loose calls is nothing like recreational humans… no bad calls a human would make.' The two structural sources of the perfection are removed: the unconditional folds at 749/772 now have a leak path, and the deterministic threshold at 816 becomes a mix. Crucially the mistakes are *visible and costly* (a station calling three streets with ace-high and losing), not cosmetic. Fresh-judge check: the errors are persona-shaped and correlated within a hand via `misread`, so they read as one player misjudging a board, not as five bots sprinkled with uniform noise.

### M3 — Two decaying mood counters plus one nemesis slot (the only source of arc) [moderate]
New per-seat session state written in `endHand` (poker-trainer.html:1249-1255) from **public outcomes only** — chips won/lost and who won them; showdown cards are explicitly not read. On each roster entry: `steam` (0..1.2), `cold` (integer), `nemesis` ({seat, heat}). Update rule: `const swing = (p.stack - r.stackAtHandStart)/BB; r.steam = Math.min(1.2, r.steam*0.75 + (swing <= -12 ? Math.min(1, -swing/40) : 0));` — so only a pot where they actually shipped ≥12bb starts steam, and it visibly decays over ~3-4 hands. `r.cold = (playedVoluntarily ? 0 : r.cold+1)`. Nemesis: when a single opponent takes a ≥12bb pot off them, `r.nemesis = {seat: winnerSeat, heat: Math.min(1, (r.nemesis.seat===winnerSeat ? r.nemesis.heat : 0)*0.8 + 0.5)}`; heat decays 0.85/hand otherwise. Pass into ctx as `mood:{steam, cold}` and `grudge` (a scalar, non-zero only when the current street aggressor *is* the nemesis seat — computed at the step() call site so botDecide never sees seat indices). Effects inside botDecide, applied as multipliers on existing dials with a per-persona `tilt` susceptibility (nit 0.4, solid 0.5, maniac 1.2, selective 0.6, station 0.9): open threshold `thr *= 1 + 0.35*steam*tilt + Math.min(0.30, 0.035*cold)`; effective bet/bluff frequency `*= 1 + 0.30*steam*tilt`; `loose *= 1 + 0.8*steam`; and vs the nemesis `nudge.callMult *= 1 + 0.35*grudge`, `nudge.raiseMult *= 1 + 0.30*grudge`. Magnitude is anchored to the tracker literature's post-loss VPIP shift (+0.10-0.12): at steam=1 a maniac widens roughly +0.13 VPIP, a nit +0.04. Separately, surface it: the seat dossier live line (`readsLiveLine`, line 855) gains one word — 'steaming' when steam>0.5, 'card-dead' when cold≥8 — so the UI does not know less about the bots than the bots do (ARCHITECTURE §12 anti-theatre doctrine). Also retune the one adaptation channel that already exists but is sub-perceptual: in `facingNudge` (684-710) raise the vpip coefficient 0.5→1.1 and the foldToBet coefficient 0.55→1.1, and use `READS_PRIOR = 12` for the *nudge* path only (leave `READS_PRIOR = 20` and the UI confidence tiers untouched — the prior is load-bearing anti-fake-certainty design for the dossier, not for the bot's own gut).

Closes: Tell 4, 'nothing drifts across 30 hands; no moods, no visible adjustment, no player having a bad night.' Every prior root cause is closed: there is now a time-varying input (steam/cold), a persistent relationship (nemesis), and the reads nudge is an order of magnitude larger so it actually flips decisions inside the observed window. Fresh-judge check: a 30-hand block will contain 2-4 steam episodes per bot, each visible as a 3-4 hand run of wider play right after that bot ships a big pot — an episode a judge can attribute to a cause they saw in the same transcript, which is exactly 'someone is having a bad night' rather than drift-shaped noise.

### M4 — Composed persona phrase bank replacing 18 inlined templates [moderate]
New pure function `reasonFor(spot)` in the BOT POLICY section; every `return` in botDecide passes a small record `{action, band, hand, pos, pct, chips, odds, draw, boardNote, readTag, readTier, mood, aggName}` and gets a string back. The string is assembled as `spotClause [+ ' ' + readClause] [+ ' ' + moodClause]`, each drawn by `Math.floor(voiceRoll*n)` from a per-persona bank added to `BOT_STYLES` as `voice:{openRaise:[…], preFold:[…], limp:[…], defend:[…], threeBet:[…], bet:[…], check:[…], callDown:[…], laydown:[…]}` — 4-6 short variants per slot per persona, roughly 100-140 flat strings total. **Truthfulness is structural, not editorial**: phrases contain no facts of their own, only slots, and every slot is filled from a value the decision actually computed — `{hand}` from `handName`, `{pct}` from the same `sHat` the decision used, `{draw}` inserted only from banks reachable when `dr.any` is true, `{odds}` verbalized from real `potOdds` ('I need about a third of the pot'), `{boardNote}` derived from the real board ('on a queen-high board'), `{chips}` from real `toCall`. A bot cannot claim a draw it does not have because the bank containing draw language is unreachable otherwise. Four hard bans, enforced by a new ~40-line `t7.js` scanning 5,000 generated reasons: no reason may contain the substring `roll `, a raw RNG value, the words `branch`/`bucket`/`clamp`/`frequency`, or a percentage narrating a 0%-probability event. Read talk is rebuilt: `facingNudge` returns `{callMult, raiseMult, tag, tier}` instead of the frozen `note` string, `tier` from `sampleTier`, and the composer emits nothing at `unknown`, a hedge at `lean` ('feels like {aggName} has been in a lot of pots'), and a committed line at `solid` ('{aggName} has been in everything tonight') — never a count. Mood clauses are only reachable when steam/cold/grudge are actually elevated, so 'I just shipped a big one to {aggName}, I'm not folding this' is a true statement about real state. Register shifts to first person throughout (0 of 753 current reasons contain 'I').

Closes: Tell 3, 'stated reasons are recycled templates… and bots quote their internal read-counters verbatim like a stats engine.' The three cited artifacts are pure text defects and are deleted outright: the printed `roll 0.90`, the code-structure names ('flat-call branch', 'the slow branch'), and the frozen ' Faces a X aggressor.' sentence. Phrasing count is unpinned from branch count — surface variety is combinatorial (spot × read × mood) rather than 18 fixed strings, and it is persona-keyed so the nit and the maniac describe the same fold differently. Fresh-judge check: the new risk is a catchphrase becoming its own tell, so the bank is written plain and short with no slang or jokes, and the acceptance metric is measured, not asserted (≥120 distinct skeletons over ~750 reasons, top-3 ≤20% vs today's 53%).

### M5 — Latency keyed to decision closeness [trivial]
In `step()` (line 1148/1166), replace the fixed `620ms` with `380 + 900*closeness + 250*Math.random()`, where `closeness` is a cheap proxy the call site can compute after the decision returns (returned on the decision object as `d.close`, 0..1, = how near `margin` was to zero, already computed by M2). Trivial folds fire in ~400ms, a river call-or-fold on a coin-flip margin takes ~1.5s. Harness-safe: `setTimeout` is replaced by a synchronously drained queue in `harness.js`/`exp-harness.js`, so no test or replay is affected.

Closes: None of the four directly — the panel read text transcripts, so this cannot move the measured score and I am stating that plainly rather than claiming credit for it. It is included because the reaction-time literature is consistent that flat latency reads as robotic in live play, and it is three lines. Drop it first if the increment gets heavy.

## Refused
- An N×N grudge matrix. One `nemesis` slot per bot carries the whole 'they're fighting each other' signal a 30-hand transcript can show; 30 pairwise counters would be state nobody can see and nobody can verify.
- A Tracery-style no-repeat / LRU guard on phrase selection. It requires per-seat text memory, which means new mutable state and a new ctx field for a purely cosmetic gain — uniform selection over 4-6 variants per slot, composed across three slots, already gives combinatorial surface variety at ~25 reasons per bot per block.
- Perlin/simplex noise for sizing jitter. At the granularity of chip counts a uniform draw and a smooth-noise draw are indistinguishable; the research value of Perlin is temporal coherence, which `chipRound` and the per-seat `sizeOpen` fingerprint already supply more legibly.
- Board-texture-conditioned frequencies (ARCHITECTURE §11 item 3). Real work, real value, but it is not a cause of any of the four tells and it would double the size of this increment.
- Mid-session style reassignment or drifting `BOT_STYLES` values. Readable fixed seat styles are the product — a bot that becomes a different player mid-session destroys the thing the learner is being trained to read.
- Any showdown-derived mood or reads. `endHand` has the revealed cards in hand and it would be one line to use them; keeping the action/card boundary clean is worth more than the fidelity (ARCHITECTURE §10).
- Weakening `READS_PRIOR` globally to make adaptation visible. The prior is load-bearing anti-fake-certainty design for the UI dossier; the nudge path gets its own lower prior instead, so a 5-sample read still cannot print a confident label.
- Any LLM in the decision or narration path. Already settled by exp/PLAN.md; the phrase bank is composed from computed values, never generated.
- Per-persona equity engines or a second decision system. `style.misread` gives bounded, characterful skill differences for one scalar and one line.

## Risks
- Exploitability regression is the real money risk. Looser calling (M2) and tilt-widened ranges (M3) change the bots' loss rates against the four probe strategies. PLAN.md criterion 3 requires always-raise / call-station / 3bet-preflop each to lose ≥300 bb/100 and check-fold to stay ≤ −15 bb/100. Loose calls should *punish* bluff-heavy probes, but `style.temp` at 0.13 for the station plus `loose:0.22` is the combination most likely to overshoot into 'exploitable by value-betting'. Re-run `exp/run-probes.js` before believing anything else.
- Persona identity could blur. M2 widens everyone's calling; if `temp` and `loose` are set too uniformly the five VPIP/F2bet profiles converge and the trainer loses its point. Criterion 2b (nit < selective < solid < min(maniac, station); nit−station F2bet ≥ 0.15; AF(maniac) − AF(station) ≥ 0.5) is the guard and must be checked, not assumed. Note that the frozen ±0.06 VPIP bands in PLAN.md pin the *old* policy and will legitimately fail — they need re-freezing after this change, which must be done deliberately and stated, not quietly.
- Faster busting shortens tables. More loose calls plus tilt means more chips move; if bots bust earlier than they do today, 30-hand blocks end up 4-handed and every rate drifts (PLAN.md already attributes the large maniac/station split-half deltas to exactly this). Watch mean players-alive at hand 30 in the baseline run.
- Reason truthfulness is the one thing that must not break, and M4's composer is where it could. The structural argument (slots only, banks gated by the branch that fills them) holds only if nobody later adds a phrase with a bare fact in it. `t7.js` catches the four banned patterns but cannot catch 'I've got a good feeling about this board' written into a fold bank. Mitigation: the bank lives in one flat table, every string is reviewed once as data, and a file-reading verifier reads all of it.
- ctx grew by six fields (`bb`, `currentBet`, `myBet`, `aggName`, `mood`, `grudge`). None are cards, but `t3.js` is mandatory and its static ban list is narrow (`isHero`, `players[0]`, `hero`, `S.`) — `aggName` in particular is a string sourced from another seat, and the reviewer should confirm it is only ever the *street aggressor's* display name, which is already printed in the log.
- Two harness contracts break loudly and must be updated in the same commit: `exp/exp-harness.js` asserts exactly 5 `Math.random()` sites (this change takes it to ~9) and does a `mustReplace` injecting `currentBet`/`minRaise`/`myBet` into ctx, which becomes a no-op once the shipped ctx carries them — `mustReplace` throws on no-op, so it fails loudly, which is correct but must be fixed rather than worked around.
- Overfitting to 16 judges. The specific hedge is that three of the four mechanisms replace an *absence* with a bounded, causally-legible behavior rather than adding noise: stable sizing fingerprints, errors correlated within a hand by `misread`, mood episodes traceable to a pot the judge watched. The failure mode to watch for in the re-panel free text is a new tell — 'these players tilt on cue' or 'the phrasing gets repetitive' — which is why the re-measure collects tells, not just a score.
